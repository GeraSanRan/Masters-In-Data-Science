About

I work with the kind of data organizations actually have: exports from management software, inconsistent labels, duplicate records, and missing fields. I like the work between the raw file and the answer, and I like explaining the result in plain language.

At a recreational sports club, I cleaned and standardized membership records and began analyzing why members renew, expire, or cancel. This portfolio collects that work along with projects from my master's program.
